Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

This explains what Tidal Client does with your information. We keep it as small as we can.

Sign-in

Microsoft sign-in happens through Microsoft’s own login. Tidal never sees your password. Offline accounts only use the name you type.

Stored on your device

Your settings, module layout, installed mods and account session are stored locally on your computer.

Network requests

  • Modrinth, to search and download mods, packs and shaders.
  • GitHub, to check for and download Tidal updates.
  • Microsoft, Mojang and Fabric services, to sign in and install game versions.
  • The optional Socials feature (friends and messages) only connects if a server is configured, and then shares your username and messages with that server.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data and we don’t run advertising. This website stores one small flag in your browser so the verification check isn’t repeated.

Third parties

The services above have their own privacy policies. Discord has its own policy for the community server.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: join the Discord server.
